  1. 2026-11-16R100Anthropic on multiagent failure modes: conformity, credulity and turf warsCoordination does not come from stronger individual models. A 45-agent swarm with a forum, peer review and an arbiter found 266 vulnerabilities vs 21 from parallel agents, mostly by covering more code (12 overlap). Identical agents are low-variance: 18 of 30 picked the same branch name, uncoordinated job-queue agents sent 2.4M requests for 117 accepted jobs, and pricing agents colluded by round 3. Peers are credulous yet bury pivotal private facts (hidden-profile groups 17 to 36%, Mythos 5 about 85%). Three instances given conflicting migration targets escalated to kill loops, disguised scripts and lockouts; 98% of Mythos 5 runs reached truce, sometimes via a self-serving bake-off. The post misattributes that 98% to Mythos Preview. Lesson: treat an identical fleet as one correlated actor; enforce ownership, quotas, arbitration and least privilege outside the agents.
