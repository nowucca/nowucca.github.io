  1. 2026-11-23R240GPU prefill, wafer-scale decode: Cerebras's heterogeneous disaggregated inferenceCerebras now splits production inference across two chip architectures: AMD Instinct GPUs do compute-bound prefill, CS-3 wafer-scale systems do bandwidth-bound decode from on-chip SRAM, and a KV Portal moves the KV cache over RDMA. On one shared GPT-OSS-120B endpoint, throughput per megawatt rose about 5x versus CS-3 only, with no loss in decode speed (vendor-measured, normalized, no time-to-first-token, traffic mix or GPU power accounting). Offered as a full stack or a decode-only SDK. The engineering questions are the cross-vendor KV format and transfer, prefill:decode ratio for agent traffic, prefix caching and doubled failure domains.
