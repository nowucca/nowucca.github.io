  1. 2026-11-11R021DeepSeek's Super-agent: one router, a ticket per taskOne long-lived main Session listens to GitHub (and Feishu), a deterministic gate decides who may start work, the model assesses the rest against team-written rules, and each accepted Ticket gets an immutable Mandate (scope, strength, policy version) and its own Executor Session and directory. Later messages reach the Ticket by subscription as context only, with no new authority. Proactive work starts in `observe` mode (record "would accept", do nothing). The post's Watchdog for loops and drift is not in the code, only an hourly liveness check; its numbers (3 to 5 sessions per person, 16 vs 29 min on one task) are press reports of the article. The value is the authority model, not the router topology.
