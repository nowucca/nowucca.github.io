  1. 2026-11-21R207Stretch evals: a practitioner's Gemini 4 Neon result on incident-investigation tasksCleric keeps a "stretch" tier of eval cases its incident-investigation agent fails today and makes it harder as models catch up. On it Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.2 Sol both score 78.8% recall (Sol about 1.5x faster and 30% cheaper); Gemini 4 Neon scores 98.1%, but is too slow and costly without its in-memory cache, which the team is tuning. A production shadow run is next. Caveats: set built from incumbent failures (selection bias favours any new model), size and run counts not given, recall only, uncached numbers. Practices: keep a stretch tier beside the main suite, promote cases once they pass, compare at equal harness and caching, gate model switches on a shadow run.
