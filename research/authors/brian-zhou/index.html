  1. 2026-11-15R078SatBench: how fast AI benchmarks saturateTreats the 173 benchmarks named in 10+ lab launch posts as one exam; an item is solved when the best reported score reaches 85%. 109 of 173 (63%) solved as of 14 Nov 2026: flat near 30% through 2024 while new tests arrived as fast as old ones fell, then +20 points in 12 months; the 2023 edition crossed 85% in Sep 2026. Share solved within a year of release: 17%, 30%, 43% for 2023-25 cohorts (median about 20 to 14 months); 14% of 2026 tests are born solved. Labs swap in harder tests, so the benchmarks they report stay about 45% solved; the 2023 canon (MMLU, HumanEval, GSM8K...) appears in 2 of 134 frontier launch posts in 2026. Benchmarks share one general factor (median rank correlation 0.67, 0.06 after Epoch's ECI). Contested first crossings: OpenAI 20, Google 14, Anthropic 14 (11 in 2026). Caveats the repo states: 75 of 100 first crossings are a lab's own launch-post claim, so independent-only it is 24%; a 10-mention admission lag undercounts 2026 tests (about 57% adjusted); projections (fixed set saturated mid-2028, live index about 2030) are extrapolations. Takeaways: public benchmarks last about a year; own an eval pipeline that makes new hard cases; separate claimed from measured; treat saturation as a trigger to raise the bar or retire.
