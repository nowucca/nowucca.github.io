  1. 2026-11-23R247Model Carving: let pretraining delete its own sublayersModel Carving learns a Transformer's architecture and weights in one pretraining run: every attention and MLP sublayer gets a learnable gate, a smooth log penalty tied to the learning rate pushes gates toward zero, and closed sublayers are removed outright, leaving a smaller, heterogeneous network. From 140M to 2.7B parameters, carved models beat dense models trained with up to 1.7x more compute and size-matched pruned or distilled ones, with up to 34% fewer FLOPs per token and 1.6x faster decoding. Caveats: unproven past 2.7B, weaker cooldown gains, harder serving for irregular layers, and data-dependent topology.
