  1. 2026-11-24R282Nango: what operating OAuth for 1,000+ APIs teaches about token refreshNango's field report from operating OAuth for about 935 providers. A May 2025 bug meant refreshed credentials were not saved; the fix was reverted in under two hours, but scheduled jobs had already thrown away tokens for hundreds of connections, and Airtable and Atlassian users had to re-consent because those providers do not accept the old refresh token. Other lessons: Airtable revokes a whole grant on a duplicate refresh, so refreshes are serialised per connection behind a distributed lock; Exact Online tokens last 10 minutes and reject refreshes more than 30 seconds early, so expiry buffers are per provider; <code>expires_in</code> is optional in RFC 6749 and Salesforce returns none; Zoho CRM returns HTTP 200 with an error body. 16% of commits to the provider config are fixes. The rules carry straight over to agents, which run unattended, fan out parallel calls on one connection, and read an empty 200 as a real answer.
