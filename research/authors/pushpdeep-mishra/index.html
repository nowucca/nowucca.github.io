  1. 2026-11-12R039LLM reliability layers sort answers; they don't fix themSix priced layers on CADEC adverse-drug-event extraction with SNOMED coding: free vocabulary check, self-correction, 3-sample voting, second-model judge, refusal to a person, the person. Layers sorted answers by trust and corrected almost none: voting (about 420K tokens a run) netted -1 to +1, self-correction fired 2 or 3 times, the judge worked only when shown the candidate menu and nothing read its verdict; dropping the paid layers changed one record. Recall of codes from memory invented 13 to 18% nonexistent codes, picking from a retrieved menu scored about 10x better. Temperature-zero runs varied by 4 F1 points for the MoE extractor only. Detection is the ceiling: no layer recovers an unquoted mention. Report yield beside accuracy. Generalized over seven corpora, except the free check reverses on gazetteers. Caveats: one task family, small open-weight models, capstone article not peer reviewed.
