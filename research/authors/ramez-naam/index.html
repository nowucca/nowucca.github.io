  1. 2026-11-24R280Ramez Naam: lab-internal task horizons sit far below the METR-style chartsRamez Naam compares the task-horizon charts used to argue that recursive self-improvement is close against the month-by-month internal figures OpenAI published and one point from Google DeepMind's Gemini 4 Argon card. At 80% success, internal horizons are about 16 minutes (OpenAI, on his adjustment) and about 9 minutes (Google), against ECI-to-METR estimates of 33x and 41x those, and AI 2027 projections of 111x and 114x; the ratio widened from 53x in June to 165x in October. At 99% success the horizon is about 28 seconds and 6 seconds. His estimate of extra research productivity per ECI point is 3.6% against a 15–19% runaway threshold. Key caveats: one lab's data for most of the series, a single Google point, his own upward adjustment to OpenAI's numbers, and curve fits extrapolated far beyond the data.
