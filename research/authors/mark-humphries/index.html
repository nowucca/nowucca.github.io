  1. 2026-11-24R259An AI model solves a 200-year-old archival attributionHistorian Mark Humphries gave pre-release GPT-6.2 Astra an open archival question: who drew an anonymous 1808 sketchbook of a canoe journey from Montreal to Fort William, held at Yale's Beinecke Library. In about 22 minutes the model ruled out the two candidates in the literature on dates, found a French aristocrat in fur-trade diaries and letters, and predicted a specific microfilmed journal would confirm it; Humphries obtained the journal and the handwriting, itinerary and a sketch entry matched. Key caveats: one case chosen by one researcher, no benchmark, and the model hedged heavily even after the evidence was decisive.
