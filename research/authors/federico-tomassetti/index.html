  1. 2026-11-24R264Is a 600K-line agent translation consistent with itself?Strumenta had Claude Code translate about 600,000 lines of IBM i RPG, DDS and CL into over a million lines of Java across 49 headless sessions, then measured consistency. Surface conventions held, but 97.9% of files were never revisited, later runtime helpers went unused by older classes, style flipped between early and late phases, and one date helper exists in 27 classes with 20 different bodies; the agent's own deterministic generators produced the most uniform code but were untested and regex-based. Key caveats: equivalence was judged by reading, not execution, and the author's company sells parser-based migration tooling.
