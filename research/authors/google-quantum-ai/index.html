  1. 2026-11-23R237Agent teams discover Lean-verified quantum programs (DeepMind, Gemini 4)Quantum circuits too large to simulate can't be scored by running them, so a Lean 4 library (quantum Hoare logic with classical variables plus resource counting) checks correctness and cost against human-written specs. Independent Gemini 4 agent teams search in parallel; only Lean-verified results enter a shared notebook, and teams meet every four hours to trade ideas. Reported against prior baselines: fusion about 12x, photochemistry 8x, Cytochrome P450 2.5x, a Grover implementation 2.57x, an arcsin routine 290x. Spec and cost-model fidelity are the trust boundary; compute is not stated and nothing ran on hardware. The library is Apache-2.0.
