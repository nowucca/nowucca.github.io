  1. 2026-11-23R232sharedMicrosoft's 12 Nov 365 outage: a four-month-old defect, a quiet failover and missing headroomA July gateway fix caused lock contention that only bit on many-core hardware under peak load. Teams failed over onto Exchange's gateway while 14% of capacity was already out; retry-to-next-healthy cascaded to over 80% of servers unresponsive in about two hours, restored servers were re-flooded, and rollback was impossible for four-month-old code, so engineers shipped a revert-to-known-good patch in waves. Lessons: load-test on production-class hardware, treat failover as a change, compute in-service headroom, admit traffic gradually on recovery.
