  3. 2026-11-12R051Six LLM serving techniques that leave the model aloneServing speed and cost are mostly won around the model: six exact techniques, each removing one waste. Prefix caching (shared-prefix KV reuse; pays only when prompts repeat), continuous batching (iteration-level admit/retire), PagedAttention (block-table KV memory, the enabler for the rest), chunked prefill (stops one long prompt from stalling decode; chunk size trades TTFT vs inter-token latency), speculative decoding (draft then verify; distribution-preserving, gain set by acceptance rate, can lose at high batch), and prefill/decode disaggregation (separate compute-bound and bandwidth-bound pools, paid for in KV transfer). Commenters: head-of-line blocking from long prefills is the common production failure; batching, paging and prefix caching cover most workloads. A primer with no numbers or citations; the summary maps each idea to its paper and adds failure modes and a review checklist.
