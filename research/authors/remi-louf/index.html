  1. 2026-11-11R027The Tool-call index: the same model breaks tool calls at very different rates by providerA weekly table, built from OpenRouter's published per-endpoint error rates, of how often each provider returns an invalid tool call (bad JSON, unknown function, schema-failing arguments) for about 49 open-weight models across 53 to 57 providers; each quantization or tier counts as its own provider. Median worst-to-best ratio about 13×; extremes of 58% (gpt-oss-120b) and 51% (Llama 3.1 8B) on single providers while the best sit under 0.1%. Ships OpenRouter routing objects (`order` the three most reliable, `ignore` at 3% or worse), an `llms.txt` and an agent skill. Harness retries hide these failures as cost and latency. Caveats: vendor that sells constrained decoding, OpenRouter traffic only, not normalized for workload, unparseable outputs not counted, syntax-valid is not correct.
