  1. 2026-11-24R27265 AIs, 127,918 answers: models give everyone the same adviceCody Collier asked 65 models from 30 companies everyday questions 127,918 times and found they converge on one favourite answer: 98% name an index fund, 90% say study computer science, 73% pick the same book. Models are poorly calibrated about it, guessing 15% would name Austin while answering Austin 80 times in 100. Siblings from the same lab cluster in answer space (59% against 4% by chance), thinking never increased variety, and saying who you are cuts the same-answer chance from 34% to 4%. In one OLMo 2 1B test the convergence appears at supervised fine-tuning. Key caveats: 50 asks per question per model, one provider per model, and the SFT finding is from a single 1B model.
