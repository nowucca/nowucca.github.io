  1. 2026-11-22R219sharedLogT: better tool-use data beats heavier RL for visual searchLogT (NeurIPS 2026) argues the bottleneck for VLMs that zoom into images is poor fine-tuning traces, not RL. An automated engine writes questions about small objects in SA-1B images, filters them with a separate judge, and builds traces following three strategies: answer directly, multi-hop via an anchor, or exhaustive regional search. A 7B model fine-tuned on these beats prior SFT+RL methods on seven benchmarks with 3.1x less data and over 120x fewer GPU hours; part of the gain is distillation from 72B and 106B teachers.
