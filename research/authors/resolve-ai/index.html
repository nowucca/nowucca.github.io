  1. 2026-11-19R155SRE-1: an RL-trained 27B model for production incident investigationQwen3.8-27B RL-trained for 200 steps (modified CISPO, no KL, ratio mask [0.2, 5]) on 1,503 real incidents plus replays, synthetic and vendor environments, through 400+ live tools across about 60 integrations, every call pinned to alert time (they still caught an agent fetching its own fix). Output is a cited causal chain scored by one of 13 frontier judges against a reference built by an audited multi-agent labeler (as good as experts on 26 of 27). Live test, 49 cases: 6.9% base to 38.8% for one investigation; a five-run ensemble hits 71%, matching Fable 5.2 at 37% less cost; Opus 5.5 five-run 65% at 4.5x cost. Offline, 73 problems: pass 51.7% vs Opus 5.5 44.5% and Fable 5.2 39.0%, but mean scores nearly tie (0.749/0.743/0.731). The ablations are the real lesson: a 25k tool-output cap (+0.16), reserving room for the final answer (+0.14) and temperature 1 (+0.12) beat every reward tweak, and a bf16 gradient-accumulation bug beat twenty reward changes. Caveats: internal evals only, test pool skewed toward cases the frontier-backed product missed, judge-defined success, uneven baseline rollout counts in SRE-1's own harness, 1,503 vs 1,549 incident count unexplained. Walks back R031's multi-agent claim: the shipped model is a single agent; the swarm became the labeler.
