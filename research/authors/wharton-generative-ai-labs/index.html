  2. 2026-11-13R065Primer: teacher-authored AI simulations through a gated multi-agent pipelineAn educator describes a lesson in a one-question-at-a-time interview; a Design Agent writes a design document (objectives, scenario flow, characters, stakes, adaptation logic) that the educator reviews and approves before anything is built; then a Casting Director, image agent and Experience Builder produce a playable simulation. Caveats: not evaluated in classrooms or reviewed for student-privacy law; "model-agnostic" but default deploy needs OpenAI Realtime and Google OAuth; no published failure rates for roleplay drift or answer leaking.
