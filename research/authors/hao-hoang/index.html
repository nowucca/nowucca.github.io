  3. 2026-11-17R131Self-Improvement Memory: carry the edit log, not just the evolved harnessContinual recursive self-improvement: an Opus 5.5-evolved harness scored below the seed harness on Gemini 3.8 Flash in all 4 domains, and harness-only continuation stayed behind a cold start in 3.
