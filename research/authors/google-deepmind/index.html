  4. 2026-11-12R038AlphaProof Code: agents formally verify Signal's protocol implementationGemini 4 Carbon agents wrote about 6.5M lines of Lean proving PQXDH plus Triple Ratchet security (forward secrecy, authentication, post-compromise security, handshake secrecy) against the stock-rustc AArch64 binary of unmodified libsignal, in symbolic and computational models; five people, four weeks, versus about twenty person-years for seL4. Specs (about 26K trusted lines incl. machine semantics) are trusted and human-approved; proofs are untrusted and kernel-checked. Roles: spec writer plus skeptical reviewers, vacuity checks, orchestrator that never edits, team leads that summarize, architect, sole-committer integrator, single-writer files, a critic (blind to proof attempts) gating small necessary spec amendments, and a gatekeeper running comparator against a held-out spec copy; 40 to 80 agents per 24 to 72h campaign. Two reintroduced old bugs were proved insecure. Caveats: spec is the trust root, crypto and some library routines are contracts, CLI harness only, no compute cost given, computational bound vacuous at scale.
