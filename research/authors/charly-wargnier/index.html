  3. 2026-11-17R122Agent Substrate: suspend idle agents, resume them on a warm poolA Kubernetes runtime that maps many stateful actors onto a small pool of pre-started gVisor or microVM worker Pods: idle actors are snapshotted (RAM and filesystem) and resumed on request by an agent-aware router, claiming 10x to 30x oversubscription and sub-500 ms resume. Its own control plane (mTLS/OIDC, OpenFGA per atespace) handles lifecycle calls Kubernetes can't; per-actor SPIFFE identity; credentials injected by an egress gateway so sandboxes and snapshots hold none; default-deny networking. Tradeoffs: snapshot data management, resume latency on the request path, a second control plane, sandbox compatibility.
