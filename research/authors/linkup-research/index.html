  1. 2026-11-24R261SPARSEUP-v2: a 150M learned sparse retriever that matches late interactionSPARSEUP-v2 is an Apache 2.0, SPLADE-style learned sparse retriever on ModernBERT-base (about 150M parameters) that turns text into weighted vocabulary terms for an inverted index. The vendor reports 57.2 nDCG@10 on BEIR-15, matching a same-size late-interaction model, plus 59.7 on MTEB English v2 retrieval, and 55.8 even when pruned to 128 terms per document. Changes from v1: sparse contrastive pre-training from scratch, cross-encoder distillation, Matryoshka top-k training and 8,192-token fine-tuning. Key caveats: vendor numbers only, English only, and it still trails late interaction on long documents (MLDR).
