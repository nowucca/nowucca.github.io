  1. 2026-11-24R279DeepCrossAttention: learned, per-token residual routing across layersDeepCrossAttention replaces the transformer's fixed residual sum with a learned router over the whole layer history: per-token weights mixing all earlier layer outputs, depth-wise attention that builds queries, keys and values from separately weighted mixes, and an initialisation identical to a plain residual network so it can be retrofitted onto a trained model. Reported results are same quality up to three times faster, an LM1B perplexity target met with 30 layers against the baseline's 42, parameter overhead under 0.2%, and a retrofit that cut perplexity by 0.19 against 0.02 for plain continued training. Key caveats: perplexity and step counts on small LM benchmarks with no downstream scores or model sizes, “up to” figures are best cases, and the extra memory bandwidth — the cost that matters for serving — is acknowledged but not quantified.
