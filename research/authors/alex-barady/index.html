  1. 2026-11-18R145Product management as one agentic funnelOnce coding, review and release run on agents, the bottleneck moves to deciding what to build; research, prioritization and delivery run on three clocks that don't sync. The fix: one funnel where agents turn signals (goals, research, customers, tech debt, analytics, last release's metrics) into a backlog and score each candidate on cost, time to market and expected move in conversion, LTV and churn; a product lead picks one feature; Gate A writes a decision record and a Product Slice (epic, stories, acceptance criteria); an agentic line builds and releases it and returns evidence; Gate B reviews outcomes, and metrics feed the next round. Maps agent coverage today: delivery typical, epics and stories partial, strategy, roadmap and business case the gap. Weak spots: consultancy pitch, no data, agent-estimated value is uncalibrated point estimates, metric scoring crowds out platform work; a commenter's fix is a falsifiable change-my-mind criterion at Gate A.
