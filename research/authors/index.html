How reputation is scored

Each public entry an author wrote or shared counts 1. An entry that passed the research Reviewer on its first round adds 0.25; one the Reviewer sent back for revision subtracts 0.25. An entry cited by a published field guide adds 0.5. Authors on the trusted sources list get their total multiplied by 1.25. Ties go to the author with more entries.
