Source: Hugging Face model card, tencent/Hy4. Official page hy.tencent.ai/research/hy4 and system card PDF. Found via TLDR AI 2026-11-23. Release date is not stated on the card; it was in the newsletter on 2026-11-23.

The model

  • Size: 770B total parameters, about 49B active per token for text (plus 1.23B vision). Instruct, FP8 and base checkpoints are published.

  • MoE: 78 layers, one dense then 77 MoE. Each MoE layer has 256 routed experts plus one shared expert; 8 routed experts are active per token.

  • Attention: Gated DeepSeek Sparse Attention on Multi-head Latent Attention, with an indexer that picks the top 2,048 tokens to attend to, plus IndexCache. This is the DeepSeek-V3.2 line of design.

  • Residuals: identity Hyper-Connections with four residual streams. This is the same family of change as Google's DeepCrossAttention (R279): more than one residual stream, mixed with learned weights.

  • Context: 1M tokens.

  • Vision: a 40-layer ViT and adapter; image-text in, text out.

  • Built-in drafter: a 5-layer, 3.3B "DFly2" speculative-decoding head ships with the model.

  • License: custom Tencent Hy License, not Apache or MIT. Read it before you build on it.

Benchmarks

Only base-model numbers are in text on the card (5-shot). Against DeepSeek-V4-Pro-Base: MMLU 91.6 vs 91.1, MMLU-Pro 73.9 vs 73.4, SuperGPQA 62.7 vs 54.8, SimpleQA 57.2 vs 52.1, U-MATH 71.6 vs 64.8. It loses on MBPP-plus (74.0 vs 79.1) and OCW Courses (66.7 vs 68.2). The instruct-model tables are images. A finance table is Tencent's own internal evaluation. The card itself says Hy4 still trails top closed models on research math, long-horizon coding and advanced visual QA.

Serving

  • vLLM image vllm/vllm-openai:hy4, tencent/Hy4-FP8 at tensor-parallel 8, speculative decoding with method: dfly and 5 draft tokens, attention backend FLASHMLA_SPARSE.

  • SGLang image lmsysorg/sglang:hy4, --tp 8 --speculative-algorithm DFLY.

  • GGUF quantisations exist (AngelSlim/Hy4-GGUF).

  • Defaults: temperature 0.9, top_p 1.0, reasoning_effort "high" or "no_think".

Why it matters for a staff engineer

Hy4 is another sign that the open-weight frontier has standardised on one recipe: large fine-grained MoE, MLA with learned sparse attention, multi-stream residuals, and a drafter shipped in the box. A single 8-GPU node in FP8 is the serving unit. For anyone running open weights (see the gap discussion in R111), the practical questions are the license, whether your serving stack has the sparse-MLA kernels and the DFly speculative path, and that the base-model wins here are knowledge benchmarks, not agentic coding, where the card admits a gap.
