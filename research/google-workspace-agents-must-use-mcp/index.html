The rule

On 24 November Google made eight Workspace MCP servers generally available and added a clause to the Workspace AI Tools Addendum of the Google APIs Terms. From 1 February 2027, any tool that "is or is driven by an AI Agent" (or an AI harness or model) must reach Gmail, Drive, Calendar and other Workspace data through Google's MCP servers, and must not disguise itself to avoid that. Breach lets Google restrict access or suspend the account. Non-AI apps are unaffected.

Related terms reported:

  • Data may be processed only to fulfil active, user-authorized tasks and must be deleted on disconnect.

  • Google may cap retention of derived "Google Technical Information" (embeddings, memory indexes, user profiles, org graphs) at 30 days.

  • No training, fine-tuning, evals or distillation on the data, and no competing Workspace product.

  • OAuth clients now carry a "Use this client for an AI-powered agent" checkbox; since 11 November the MCP servers reject clients without it. Service accounts with domain-wide delegation and Apps Script's built-in client can't set it.

Google hasn't said who qualifies for partner access. The article says Anthropic's Workspace connector advertises send, reply, forward and Drive move/trash, which this entry did not verify.

Quotas and pricing

Agents get a Standard tier of 5,000 MCP requests per day per project per product, with a Gmail search_threads or list_drafts call counting as 10. Paid Scaled-tier billing for Gmail, Calendar and Drive starts 1 April 2027; REST keeps large free allowances but, per the article, falling back to REST from an agent after 1 February breaches the terms. Google hasn't said how it will detect undeclared agents.

Engineering takeaways

  • Agent builders on Workspace lose direct REST as an option and inherit Google's tool surface, quotas and retention rules.

  • The 30-day cap on derived data hits agent memory designs that keep embeddings or profiles of mail and documents.

  • A read-mostly Gmail surface (search, read, draft) is what Google is willing to give agents by default; sending stays gated.
