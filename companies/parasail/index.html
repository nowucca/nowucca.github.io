Company profile

Parasail

Pay-per-token inference cloud for AI-native startups, brokering GPU capacity across ~40 data centers plus its own fleet.

Researched 2026-11-05WebsiteLinkedInXDocs

Scope: this is Parasail Inc. of San Mateo, CA (parasail.io), not the older parasail.com or unrelated "Parasail" brands. LinkedIn company pages and SEC EDGAR were read on 2026-11-05 with the company-research scripts. Glassdoor could not be read, and no employee reviews turned up in search.

Snapshot

Parasail sells open-model inference (Kimi, GLM, DeepSeek, MiniMax, gpt-oss and 40+ others in its library) through serverless per-token endpoints, "elastic" priority endpoints, dedicated reserved GPUs and a discounted batch tier. Its pitch is "commit to spend, not GPUs": it aggregates capacity from about 40 third-party data centers in 15 countries and spot "liquidity markets" alongside its own Hopper and Blackwell fleet, then routes and tunes workloads automatically. Customers are mostly seed-to-Series-B AI startups (Elicit, mem0, Venice, Gravity, Kotoba, Reflow). It is a ~2½-year-old private company that has raised about $42M, with 49 people on LinkedIn (doubled in a year), and it competes with Together AI, Fireworks AI and Baseten.

Mission, purpose, vision, values

No formal mission or values list is published. The closest statements, verbatim:

  • Tagline: "The Inference Cloud for AI-native startups" (homepage); on LinkedIn: "The multi-silicon inference cloud." running across NVIDIA, AMD, d-Matrix, Positron and other chips (LinkedIn)
  • Vision, "Building for the Token Economy": "In the next few years, inference will become the largest computing workload on earth." and "AI-native companies need a token economy, not a hardware economy: commit to output, not silicon, on a fluid fleet that flexes with you." (About)
  • How they operate: "Transparency is the foundation." with "no silent quantization, no capacity that vanishes the moment you need it." (About)
  • CEO Mike Henry: "We're relentless about delivering the best inference performance per dollar." (d-Matrix release, 2026-07-08)

History

  • 2023: Founded, per Built In, by Mike Henry (founder of analog AI chip maker Mythic in 2012, later interim Chief Product Officer at Groq, where he set up its cloud service) and Tim Harris (founder and CEO of Swift Navigation). Operated in stealth.
  • 2025-04-02: Launched publicly as "the First AI Deployment Network" with a $10M seed and named customers Elicit, Rasa, Everpilot, Weights & Biases and Oumi. Pitch then: 15–30x cheaper than OpenAI/Anthropic, H100/H200/A100/4090 capacity (Business Wire).
  • 2026-04-15: $32M Series A; branding shifted to "AI Supercloud" for agents, claiming 500B tokens/day and 30% month-over-month revenue growth (PR Newswire).
  • 2026-07-08: Partnership to run d-Matrix Corsair accelerators for decode beside NVIDIA GPUs for prefill ("heterogeneous disaggregated inference"); repositioned as "the inference cloud for AI-native startups", inference only.
  • Late 2026: Homepage now claims "Trillions of Tokens served daily" (unverified; up from 500B/day in April). Hiring for data center deployment and a European SRE site lead suggests the owned fleet is growing.

Ownership and status

Private, venture-backed. Legal name reported as "Parasail Inc." (SiliconANGLE); jurisdiction not confirmed. EDGAR has no Form D filed by Parasail itself (searched name variants and the HQ address on 2026-11-05). HQ 4 W 4th Ave, San Mateo, CA 94402 per Built In; press releases are datelined San Francisco. No parent company. A job posting says it is a federal contractor (VEVRAA/Section 503 forms).

Signals for a candidate

Green flags

  • Riding the biggest demand curve in AI infra: inference volume claimed to grow from billions to trillions of tokens/day in 18 months.
  • Experienced founder: Henry has built an AI chip company (Mythic) and ran product and cloud at Groq.
  • Capital-light model (brokered capacity, no long-term GPU commitments) limits the stranded-hardware risk that sank some GPU clouds.
  • Headcount doubled in a year (16 to 49 on LinkedIn since Oct 2024), with engineering hiring accelerating.
  • Early enough (~50 people) for broad ownership and real equity upside; direct access to the CEO.
  • Serious systems work: kernels, scheduling, heterogeneous hardware, multi-region reliability.

Red flags and risks

  • Crowded, price-driven market (Together AI, Fireworks, Baseten, DeepInfra, plus hyperscalers and model labs selling their own APIs); token prices keep falling.
  • Small war chest ($42M) for a business that is now also running its own Hopper/Blackwell fleet and hiring data center ops.
  • Positioning has shifted three times in 18 months ("AI Deployment Network" → "AI Supercloud" for agents and training → "Inference Cloud for AI-native startups").
  • Customer concentration risk with startup customers who churn or get acquired; several homepage logos (Z, MiniMax, Kimi) look like model partners rather than paying customers.
  • No public CTO/VP Eng; co-founder Tim Harris also runs Swift Navigation. Metrics (tokens/day, 30% MoM) are unaudited company claims.

Questions to ask

  1. What share of tokens runs on owned GPUs vs. brokered capacity, and what are gross margins on each?
  2. Revenue run-rate and growth since April; runway at current burn; plans for the next raise or debt for hardware?
  3. Who leads engineering, how is the team organized, and how big is it today?
  4. Top-10 customer concentration, and how much revenue comes from model labs vs. app companies?
  5. How does on-call and reliability work across third-party data centers you don't control?
  6. What's the hybrid expectation in San Mateo, and how is the Europe team set up?
