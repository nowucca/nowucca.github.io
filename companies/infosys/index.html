Company profile

Infosys

Bengaluru-based IT services and consulting giant (NYSE: INFY), about 332,500 staff, betting on AI services while growth stalls.

Researched 2026-11-19WebsiteLinkedInInvestors

This is the parent, Infosys Limited (Bengaluru, India). Subsidiaries such as Infosys BPM, EdgeVerve (Finacle), Infosys Public Services and the 2026 acquisitions Optimum Healthcare IT and Stratus are covered only as parts of the group. The LinkedIn company script was not run for this profile, and the stock price and market cap are as of late October 2026.

Snapshot

Infosys is India's second-largest IT services firm. It sells consulting, application development and maintenance, cloud and infrastructure management, BPO and software products (Finacle core banking, via EdgeVerve) to large enterprises, mostly in North America and Europe, and is paid on time-and-materials and fixed-price contracts. It is a mature public company with revenue of about US$19 billion in FY26 (year to March 2026) and 332,542 employees at 2026-09-30. Growth has nearly stopped (FY27 guidance: 1% to 2% in constant currency), and the 2026 story is AI: the stock fell sharply on fears that AI agents will shrink outsourcing work, and Infosys is answering with its Topaz AI platform and partnerships with Anthropic, Cognition and Google Cloud.

Mission, purpose, vision, values

  • Purpose: "To amplify human potential and create the next opportunity for people, businesses and communities" (Our Values, careers site).
  • Values (C-LIFE): Client Value: "To surpass client expectations consistently with our hunger and boldness". Leadership by Example: "To act on our conviction that ordinary people can be inspired and mentored to do extraordinary things". Integrity and Transparency: "To be ethical, sincere and open in all our transactions". Fairness: "To be objective, empathetic and caring in our transactions". Excellence: "To strive relentlessly, constantly learn, improve ourselves, our teams, and services to become the best" (same page).
  • Mission and vision: no current mission or vision statement was found on infosys.com. The one on Comparably is for "Infosys International Inc." and looks out of date.

History

  • 1981: founded 2 July in Pune as Infosys Consultants Private Limited by N. R. Narayana Murthy, Nandan Nilekani, Kris Gopalakrishnan, S. D. Shibulal, K. Dinesh, N. S. Raghavan and Ashok Arora; moved to Bangalore in 1983 (Wikipedia).
  • 1992: renamed Infosys Technologies; renamed Infosys Limited in 2011.
  • 1999: ADRs listed on Nasdaq on 11 March, the first Indian company listed there (Form F-1); moved the ADS listing to NYSE in 2012.
  • 2002 to 2014: founder-CEOs in turn: Murthy to 2002, Nilekani 2002 to 2007, Gopalakrishnan 2007 to 2011, Shibulal 2011 to 2014. Infosys BPM began as Progeon in 2002; EdgeVerve was set up in 2014.
  • 2013: paid US$34 million to settle a US civil case over visa use, without admitting guilt.
  • 2014 to 2017: Vishal Sikka, the first non-founder CEO, bought Panaya (US$200M, 2015) and resigned in August 2017 after a public dispute with the founders. U. B. Pravin Rao was interim CEO.
  • 2018: Salil Parekh (ex-Capgemini) became CEO on 2 January; Nandan Nilekani returned as chairman in 2017.
  • 2020 to 2024: acquisitions including Simplus (US$250M, 2020), InSemi (₹280 crore, 2024) and in-tech (€450M, 2024).
  • 2025: largest-ever buyback, ₹18,000 crore at ₹1,800 a share, approved 2025-09-12 (Outlook Business).
  • 2026: bought Optimum Healthcare IT (up to US$465M) and Stratus (up to US$95M, P&C insurance tech), both announced 2026-03-25 (Yahoo Finance, Infosys).

Ownership and status

Public. Infosys Limited is incorporated in India and listed on the NSE and BSE (INFY), with American Depositary Shares on the NYSE (INFY). As a foreign private issuer it files an annual Form 20-F with the SEC rather than a 10-K; the FY26 20-F was announced on 2026-06-15 (press release). The founders' families still hold a minority stake; most shares are held by institutions. The ADS closed at US$12.66 on 2026-10-23, near the bottom of a 52-week range of US$10.12 to US$30 (SIA). Market cap was about ₹4.9 lakh crore on 2026-04-27 after it dropped out of India's 10 most valuable companies (Kotak Neo). At October's price it is very roughly US$50 billion (estimate: about 4.1 billion shares).

Financials

  • FY26 (year to 2026-03-31): revenue ₹178,803 crore (about US$19 billion), net income ₹29,474 crore (Wikipedia infobox); constant-currency growth 3.1%; 96 large deals worth US$14.9 billion TCV; AI-related revenue about US$1 billion a year (People Matters, 2026-06-24).
  • Q2 FY27 (quarter to 2026-09-30, reported 2026-10-23): revenue ₹49,244 crore / US$5.145 billion, +10.7% YoY in rupees but only +1.3% in dollars and +1.5% in constant currency (+1.2% QoQ). Operating margin 21.0%. Net profit ₹7,823 crore (+6.2% YoY), slightly below analyst estimates; in dollars, net profit fell 2.5% to US$819M. Large-deal TCV US$5.2 billion, 62% net new (Free Press Journal, SIA).
  • Guidance cut: FY27 constant-currency growth lowered to 1% to 2% from 1.5% to 3% (it started the year at 1.5% to 3.5%); operating margin guidance held at 20% to 22%.
  • AI revenue: 10.5% of revenue in Q2 FY27, up 29% QoQ (CEO Salil Parekh), versus 5.5% (about US$275M) in the December 2025 quarter (TechCrunch). The definition of "AI revenue" is Infosys's own.
  • Segments in Q2: growth in financial services, energy and utilities, communications and life sciences; declines in manufacturing, retail and hi-tech.

Engineering

  • Work is organized by industry vertical (financial services, retail, communications, energy, manufacturing, hi-tech, life sciences) and by service line, delivered by project teams for clients rather than on Infosys's own products. US roles are mostly client-facing: technology lead, architect, delivery manager, consultant.
  • Platforms: Topaz (AI services and agents), Cobalt (cloud), Finacle (core banking, EdgeVerve), Panaya (SAP/Oracle change testing).
  • AI partnerships in 2026: Cognition (Devin), announced 2026-01-07 (press release); Anthropic, 2026-02-17, putting Claude models into Topaz for banking, telecom and manufacturing agents, with Claude Code used internally (TechCrunch); an expanded Google Cloud tie-up (IT Brief); Intel (March 2026). Its "AI First Value Framework" claims a US$300 billion AI services market (press release).
  • Example US opening: Principal Tech Architect, telecom OSS/BSS, Basking Ridge NJ, US$112,725 to US$160,698, 15+ years, on-site, no visa sponsorship (posting, 2025-10-17). Postings are on digitalcareers.infosys.com.

Culture and reviews

  • Glassdoor: 3.6 of 5 from about 122,000 reviews; 67% would recommend; 72% approve of CEO Salil Parekh; 66% positive outlook. The IT-industry average is 3.7 (Glassdoor, read 2026-11-19). Most reviews are from India.
  • Recurring themes: lots of learning and project variety, travel; slow or "non-existent" raises, slow promotion, a hierarchy where long tenure blocks advancement.
  • US pay (Levels.fyi, updated 2026-11-11): software engineer median total comp US$100,000; JL5 Technology Lead US$103K, ranging to about US$142K at JL6A (Levels.fyi). Technology Architect base about US$111K on PayScale (14 profiles, data from 2022). Pay is well below product companies; equity is minor.
  • In June 2026 Infosys suspended recruitment assessments for 20,000+ candidates over impersonation; in July 2026 a French labour authority fined it €175,000 (Wikipedia).

Signals for a candidate

Green flags

  • Very stable: profitable (21% operating margin), cash-rich, returning cash to shareholders, still signing US$5 billion a quarter in large deals.
  • Real momentum in AI work (AI revenue 10.5% of total and growing about 29% a quarter), with Anthropic, Cognition and Google partnerships. An agent-delivery architect or FDE-style role fits that push.
  • Huge client base in the US, so there is breadth of domains (banking, telecom, insurance, healthcare after Optimum and Stratus).

Red flags and risks

  • Growth has stalled (1% to 2% guidance, cut twice in 2026) and the stock lost more than half its value from its 52-week high, so cost pressure on US roles is likely.
  • The core business is exactly what AI agents threaten; it is a services company, so engineers build for clients, not their own product.
  • US pay is low for senior engineers (US$100K to US$160K bands), with little equity and slow raises per reviews.
  • Immigration and labour scrutiny: PERM suspension in October 2026, past visa settlements, heavy H-1B use. Expect policy and political risk around US staffing.
  • No Austin hub: US roles are mostly on-site near client or hub locations (e.g. Richardson TX, New Jersey).

Questions to ask

  1. Which client or account is this role on, and how long is that engagement contracted for?
  2. How is AI changing delivery on your projects: are teams getting smaller, and how is pricing moving from time-and-materials to outcomes?
  3. What does Topaz look like in practice: is there a product team building agents, or is it a brand over project work?
  4. Where would I be based, and how much on-site client time is expected? Is remote from Austin possible?
  5. What job level (JL) is this, what is the pay band, and how have raises and promotions worked in the last two cycles?
