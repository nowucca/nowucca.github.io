Verified Oct 2026

  • Private. Last priced round: $200M Series D (2021), ~$3B valuation. No IPO filing found.
  • $200M+ ARR, 25%+ YoY growth, a quarter of the Fortune 500 (GlobeNewswire, 2026-08-06).
  • AI pivot: "runtime control layer for agentic AI". AgentControl (launched 2026-05-19) manages prompts/models/tools/policies as runtime config, with guarded rollouts, benchmarking, tracing, and <200ms propagation. AI pipeline nearly doubled after launch. 50T flag evaluations a day.

Background (unverified)

  • Founded 2014 by Edith Harbaugh and John Kodumal; HQ Oakland, CA. Remote-friendly.
  • CEO: co-founder Edith Harbaugh (per Aug 2026 release; Dan Rogers was CEO from 2023). CTO Cameron Etezadi. New CRO Andy Pemberton (ex-OutSystems, Aug 2026).

  • Product: feature management platform (feature flags, progressive delivery, targeting), plus experimentation, "guarded releases" (auto-rollback on regressions), AI Configs (managing prompts/models at runtime), and observability/session replay (Highlight acquisition, 2025).

  • Positioning: "release software safely" / decouple deploy from release; reduce blast radius.

  • Funding: ~$330M total; Series D $200M (2021, ~$3B valuation). Notable investors: Bessemer, Threshold, Redpoint, Uncork.
  • Customers: thousands of enterprises (e.g., IBM, Atlassian-era refs, large banks/airlines).
  • Competitors: Statsig (acquired by OpenAI 2025), Split (acquired by Harness), Optimizely, Unleash, GrowthBook, Flagsmith.
