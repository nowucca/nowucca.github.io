ParentSquare

K-12 school-to-home communication platform (messaging, alerts, attendance, school websites, payments), owner of Remind. Founder-led, backed by Serent Capital, remote-first.

Snapshot

ParentSquare sells a unified family-engagement platform to US school districts: two-way messaging with translation into 190+ languages, mass and emergency alerts, attendance outreach (Attendance Plus), school websites (Smart Sites), payments, forms and secure document delivery, plus the Remind teacher-messaging app it bought in 2023. Districts pay licence fees (pricing not public). It reports more than 22 million students and 42,000+ schools in all 50 states, about 400 employees, a 99.4% customer retention rate, and four straight years on the Inc. 5000. It is private, founder-run (CEO Sohit Wadhwa, President Anupama Vaid) and majority growth-equity backed by Serent Capital since 2021 (Santa Barbara Independent, 2026-08-12).

History

  • 2011: Founded in Santa Barbara by Anupama Vaid and her husband Sohit Wadhwa. Vaid wanted an easier way to hear from her children's school; a principal had three teachers help shape the first version. Early customers Evergreen Learning Center, then Santa Barbara Unified (Independent).
  • 2011–2019: Grew mostly by word of mouth; raised small seed rounds.
  • 2020-03: Acquired Signal Kit (Kansas City, community messaging for districts); its KC team later doubled (Startland News).
  • 2020-12: $7M additional seed round; "more than $12 million over five funding rounds", 4M+ users in 44 states (PRWeb).
  • 2021-08: Serent Capital growth investment, undisclosed (Business Wire).
  • 2023-01: Acquired Gabbart Communications (district websites, public engagement) and launched Smart Sites (THE Journal).
  • 2023-12: Acquired Remind (San Francisco, founded 2011, ~$59.5M VC-raised; used in 80%+ of US public schools). Terms undisclosed; Remind keeps its brand, Remind Tutoring shut down (Business Wire, EdWeek Market Brief).
  • 2023–2026: Inc. 5000 four years running (2024: No. 763, 665% three-year growth; 2025: No. 962, 444%; 2026: No. 788).
  • 2026-03: Launched ParentSquare Intelligence, an AI and data layer across the platform.

Ownership and status

Private. ParentSquare, Inc. (state of incorporation not confirmed). Serent Capital (Austin/SF growth equity, ~$5B AUM) has been the institutional backer since 2021; Serent partner Lance Fenton sits on the board, and the Executive Chairman is Lane Rankin. Founders Wadhwa and Vaid remain on the board and run the company. Board: Lane Rankin (Executive Chairman), Lance Fenton, Tom Kenny, Anupama Vaid, Sohit Wadhwa; observers Dexter Hopen, Tory Patterson (Our Team). Whether Serent holds a majority is not disclosed, but a PE-led board with an executive chairman usually means a control or near-control stake, and an eventual exit (sale to a strategic or another sponsor) is the likely path rather than an IPO.

Engineering

  • Stack (from job ads): Ruby, Python, JavaScript/TypeScript and PHP; MySQL/PostgreSQL; AWS (Aurora, RDS, SQS); message queues; REST APIs. The AI Platform role says it "works in the existing monolith" and "there is no fixed stack", which points to a Rails-era monolith plus code inherited from acquisitions (Remind, Gabbart's PHP websites, Signal Kit) (Engineer posting).
  • Scale: 22M+ students, messaging plus SMS/voice/email fan-out, emergency alerts, and sharp back-to-school and weather-day peaks. Public status page shows recurring incidents (login failures, slowness, Smart Sites outages) (status.parentsquare.com, IsDown history).
  • AI: ParentSquare Intelligence (2026-03) with Contactability Benchmark, AI Rewrite, AI Alt Text; AI Assistant for Smart Sites and Conversation Starters (2026-08). No model vendor named.

Signals for a candidate

Green flags

  • Category leader with real distribution: 22M students, 42K schools, Remind's teacher reach, 99%+ retention, sticky district contracts.
  • Sustained growth under PE backing: four years on the Inc. 5000 without new venture rounds (profitability not disclosed).
  • Principal Engineer opening ($210K–$265K, remote) explicitly about scale, reliability and AI-assisted engineering, reporting into the VP Eng with exec exposure.
  • Fresh AI push (ParentSquare Intelligence, new AI platform team) gives a senior engineer room to set direction.
  • Remote-first, good work/life scores, founders still in charge.

Red flags and risks

  • PE ownership since 2021: an exit in the next 1–3 years is likely, which can bring cost cuts or a new owner. Ask how options pay out on a sale.
  • Technical debt from consolidation: a monolith plus three acquired codebases in several languages (Ruby, PHP, Python, JS); recurring public incidents.
  • Comp complaints on Glassdoor (3.6/5) and capped raises; base for principal is fair but equity in a PE-backed company is usually modest.
  • Seasonal, budget-dependent customers: K-12 spending depends on district budgets, and federal pandemic relief (ESSER) money has run out (general context, not company-specific).
  • No CTO listed; tech leadership split between a CIO and the VP Engineering.
  • Small Glassdoor sample (39), mostly non-engineering.

Questions to ask

  1. How is engineering organised after Remind, Gabbart and Signal Kit, and what is the plan for the monolith and the acquired stacks?
  2. What does the Principal Engineer own in the first year: reliability at peak (back-to-school, emergency alerts), AI platform, or platform consolidation?
  3. Who would I report to, and how do the CIO and VP Engineering split technical decisions?
  4. How big is engineering, and what share is in AI work now?
  5. What's the reliability picture: SLOs, on-call load, and lessons from recent incidents?
  6. How do stock options work in a sponsor-backed company, and what happens to them in a sale?
  7. How exposed is revenue to district budget cuts, and how is net revenue retention trending?
  8. What models or vendors power ParentSquare Intelligence, and how is student data privacy (FERPA, COPPA, state laws) handled in AI features?
