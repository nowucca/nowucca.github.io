Company profile

BetterUp

Enterprise coaching platform (human coaches plus an AI coach) for leadership, manager and workforce development. Private, Austin TX, about 1,900 to 2,700 staff.

Researched 2026-11-16WebsiteLinkedInGitHub

Legal entity is BetterUP, Inc. (Delaware, SEC CIK 1664032). Not BetterHelp, Better.com or Betterment. LinkedIn company data (headcount by function, growth insights, open roles) was not gathered this run, and Glassdoor's own page could not be read, so culture figures come from aggregators.

Snapshot

BetterUp sells coaching to employers as a subscription: one-on-one and group sessions with a network of 4,000+ contract coaches in 70+ countries, assessments, and since 2025 an AI coach that runs alongside the humans (About). Products are BetterUp Lead (leadership development), Manage (manager effectiveness), AI Coaching (for every employee) and Ready (workforce resilience, formerly Care). It claims 750+ enterprise customers, including Meta, Salesforce, Hilton and the US Air Force (Wikipedia). It is a late-stage private company that last priced a round in 2021 at $4.7B, has raised about $567M, and has an estimated $150M to $250M in revenue (estimates disagree; see Financials).

Mission, purpose, vision, values

  • Mission: "BetterUp exists to help people live with greater clarity, purpose, and energy at work and to help organizations turn that human capacity into sustained performance." (About us, read 2026-11-16)
  • "That mission shows up in how we build the product, how we coach, and how we measure success." (About us)
  • Origin story: "BetterUp was born when co-founder Alexi Robichaux hit burnout as a young executive and discovered how powerful coaching could be." (About us)
  • No separate vision or values statement is published on the About page or linked from it.

History

  • 2013: Founded by Alexi Robichaux (CEO) and Eduardo Medina (COO) (Wikipedia).
  • 2016: Public launch and mobile app, with seed and Series A money ($12.9M combined) (Wikipedia).
  • 2018: Series B ($26M); BetterUp Labs research arm launched.
  • 2019: $103M Series C.
  • 2021: $125M Series D at $1.7B (Feb), $100M ARR and European offices (Jul), $300M Series E at $4.7B (Oct). Prince Harry joined as Chief Impact Officer (Mar). Acquired Motive (emotion detection in language) and Impraise (manager tooling).
  • 2022: More than 600 contract coaches reportedly objected to proposed pay changes (IBTimes).
  • 2023: Laid off 16% of staff, more than 100 people, in August (Daily Beast). Acquired Crescent (AI sleep coach). Reported $99M US Air Force coaching contract.
  • 2024–2025: Acquired Heyday (AI for coaches) and Practica (AI skills coaching); launched a standalone AI coaching app in August 2025 and a "Human + AI Coaching for All" suite in October 2025 (Wikipedia, Sacra).
  • HQ: Started in San Francisco; press releases and EDGAR now give 3100 E 5th St, Austin TX, with a second office in Toronto.

Ownership and status

Private. BetterUP, Inc., a Delaware corporation, CIK 1664032, business address 3100 E 5th St, Suite 350, Austin TX (SEC EDGAR, read 2026-11-16). No ticker and no 10-K or S-1. Venture-backed with no parent; investors include ICONIQ Growth, Lightspeed, Wellington Management, Salesforce Ventures, Sapphire Ventures, Mubadala Capital, Morningside and PLUS Capital. A Canadian subsidiary, BetterUp Canada Inc., runs the Toronto operation.

Financials

  • BetterUp does not publish revenue; its Form Ds say "Decline to Disclose".
  • Company-stated: $100M ARR in July 2021 (Business Wire).
  • Estimates: about $125M ARR at the Series E, October 2021 (Sacra); $175M ARR end-2022, about 40% growth (Sacra); $250M for 2023 (Sacra headline figure, model estimate) (Sacra).
  • Estimates: $151.7M (Nov 2023) and $214.6M (Oct 2024) (GetLatka, updated 2026-10-07).
  • In 2023 BetterUp reportedly fell more than 20% short of its revenue targets for the prior fiscal year (two unnamed sources, Daily Beast).
  • Profitability: not found. The May 2026 hire of a public-company CFO who also runs investor relations is a possible sign of IPO or sale preparation (inference).

Engineering

  • Org: CTO Amol Kher; AI leadership includes Jordan Hochenbaum (VP, Artificial Intelligence), Samiur Rahman (VP, AI Product) and Andrew Reece (Chief AI Scientist). Product sits under SVPs Manoj Sharma (Product Management) and Allison Yost (Product Strategy), with David Garver as VP Product Management (Platform). No separate head of engineering is listed (Leadership team).
  • Stack (from the GitHub org, 76 public repos): Ruby/Rails is the core (most-starred repos are Ruby gems such as devise-doorkeeper, wisper-activerecord-publisher, boolean_timestamps, and a fork of Microsoft's kiota-http-ruby). Platform forks point to Kubernetes and GitOps (Helm, Argo CD, Kyverno, sops, cluster autoscaler), Go tooling, and LLM work in Python and TypeScript (langflow, a LibreChat fork, codex-cli-subagents, natural-turn-transcription). Several repos were updated in the week of this report. Fork activity suggests, but doesn't prove, internal use.
  • AI product: AI coach with long-term memory (three-part blog series "Remembering on purpose: Inside our AI coach's memory"), Microsoft 365 Copilot integration ("Work IQ in Copilot and M365", Fall 2026 release) and a Microsoft collaboration for managers (Blog, Press).
  • Engineering blog: eng.betterup.com exists but blocks crawlers; the main blog is product and research marketing.
  • Open roles: TrueUp counts 7 open roles, 1 in software engineering (TrueUp); Revelio reports 196 postings in 2026 (up 49%), which probably includes coach recruiting. Jobs are on Ashby (jobs.ashbyhq.com/betterup).

Culture and reviews

  • Aggregate (TrueUp across Glassdoor, Blind and its own data, 400+ reviews): 50% would recommend, 54% approve of CEO Alexi Robichaux (TrueUp). Glassdoor's own page could not be read this run.
  • Recurring themes in recent Glassdoor review titles: "A sinking ship with novice (and ego driven) captains", "A Company with Incredible Potential Held Back by Leadership", "Great mission, but all over the place", "Toxic train wreck", and from coaches "Meaningful work but zero raises and no benefits". Praise centers on the mission and benefits.
  • 2023 reporting described executive outbursts, unexplained departures, "toxic positivity" and aggressive sales targets; three ex-employees called the CEO capricious (Daily Beast, recapped by IBTimes 2026-11-10). Anonymous sources; BetterUp declined to comment.
  • Revelio rates employee sentiment as negative and declining (Revelio, Aug 2026).
  • Pay: Levels.fyi software engineer median total comp $195K (20 submissions, US; range $163K to $270K; senior SF example base $195K, stock $0 shown). Equity vests over 4 years, 25% after year 1, then monthly (Levels.fyi, updated 2026-10-21).
  • Benefits: $1K learning stipend, $600 wellness allowance, 4 paid "Inner Work" days and 5 volunteer days, flexible time off, 401K, parental leave, $20K family-planning stipend, and BetterUp coaching for staff (Careers).

Signals for a candidate

Green flags

  • Category leader in enterprise coaching with large customers (Meta, Salesforce, US Air Force) and an AI coach shipped and integrated with Microsoft 365 Copilot.
  • Real applied-LLM work: AI coach memory, transcription, agent tooling visible on GitHub.
  • Headcount has been stable since 2023, with no layoffs found in 2025 or 2026.
  • New public-company CFO in 2026 suggests a push toward an exit (inference).

Red flags and risks

  • No priced round since 2021 and an indicative secondary valuation of $0.9B, down 81% from $4.7B. Option or RSU value at the old preference stack may be small.
  • Weak culture signals: 50% recommend and 54% CEO approval across 400+ reviews, negative Revelio sentiment, and repeated complaints about leadership.
  • 2023 layoff (16%) and a reported revenue miss; revenue estimates since then imply slower growth.
  • AI coaching is commoditizing fast; the core business depends on contract coaches who have pushed back on pay.
  • Very few engineering openings right now (1 of 7 roles).

Questions to ask

  1. What is current ARR and growth, and is the company profitable or how many months of runway does it have?
  2. What is the latest 409A strike price compared with the Series E price, and what is the liquidation preference stack? Are there tender offers?
  3. Is an IPO or sale the plan, and on what timeline, given the new CFO?
  4. How big is engineering, how is it organized under the CTO, and how much of it works on the AI coach versus the Rails platform?
  5. How does the AI coach split revenue and usage with human coaching, and how do you compete with general assistants like Copilot?
  6. What has changed in leadership and culture since 2023, and what is engineering attrition?
  7. Remote policy: is the role Austin, Toronto or remote?
