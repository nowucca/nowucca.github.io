The Austin Spiral

Three ways to run a night. The Spiral is a downtown radar of every saved bar, wound into one inward coil. Playbooks are ready-made district crawls — venue by venue, in walking order, with the timing worked out. By category starts from the mood instead — cocktails, speakeasies, live music, rooftops and more — and lists the matches nearest downtown first. This Week and Events refresh daily with openings, comedy, live music, trivia and bar nights. Tap any venue name to open its site.

How the order works

Center is downtown (Congress at the lake). Stops are wound by a spiral key — sweeping through the compass while the radius steps down — so you start at the farthest bar and coil inward, ending in the core. Direction and mileage are real; only the ring spacing is compressed.
