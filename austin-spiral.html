The Austin Spiral

Three ways to run a night. The Spiral winds every saved spot (bars, music rooms, arcades, duckpins, rooftops) into one inward night crawl, from the far edges of town to the core (flip to Map for real directions). Playbooks are ready-made district crawls — venue by venue, in walking order, with the timing worked out. By category starts from the mood instead — cocktails, speakeasies, live music, rooftops and more — and lists the matches nearest downtown first. This Week collects the best one-off nights, grouped by type, and Events lists everything day by day; both refresh daily. Tap any venue name to open its site.

How the order works

Stops are wound by a spiral key, sweeping through the compass while the distance from downtown steps down, so you start at the farthest spot and coil inward to the core. Spiral lays that order out evenly along one coil; Map shows the real direction from downtown with distance compressed. The list gives true direction and miles either way.
