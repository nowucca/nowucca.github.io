Big-batch green bean casserole

The classic Campbell's-style casserole scaled up about 20% to fill a large 13×11 foil pan. Amounts below are per tray; double for two.

  • Pan13×11 / large foil pan
  • Oven350°F
  • Bake~25 min + 5

Per tray

  • 5 canscut green beans (14.5 oz each), drained
  • 2½ canscondensed cream of mushroom soup (10.5 oz)
  • 1¼ cupsmilk
  • 2½ tspsoy sauce
  • to tasteblack pepper
  • ~3¼ cupsFrench fried onions, divided

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. In the pan, stir together the soup, milk, soy sauce and pepper.
  3. Fold in the drained green beans and half the fried onions.
  4. Bake about 25 minutes, until hot and bubbling.
  5. Stir, top with the remaining onions, and bake about 5 minutes more until the onions are golden.
