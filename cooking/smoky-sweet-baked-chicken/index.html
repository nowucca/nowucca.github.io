Baked chicken with smoky-sweet rub

Boneless skinless chicken breasts with a smoked paprika and brown sugar rub, baked hot so they stay juicy. Easy to double for a crowd, and great with baked mac and cheese.

  • Makes~3 lb chicken (6–8 servings)
  • Oven425°F
  • Bake20–25 min
  • Done at165°F inside

Rub (per ~3 lb chicken)

  • 1 Tbspsmoked paprika
  • 1 Tbsplight brown sugar
  • 2 tspgarlic powder
  • 2 tsponion powder
  • 1½ tspsalt
  • 1 tspblack pepper

Also

  • ~3 lbboneless skinless chicken breasts
  • ~2 Tbspolive oil

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Pound any thick breasts to an even thickness (about ¾ inch) so they cook at the same rate.
  3. Stir the rub ingredients together in a small bowl.
  4. Toss the chicken in the olive oil, then press the rub onto all sides.
  5. Lay the pieces in a single layer on a sheet pan or in a baking dish.
  6. Bake 20–25 minutes, until the thickest piece reads 165°F on an instant-read thermometer.
  7. Rest 5 minutes before slicing.

Cooking for a crowd

For about 6 lb, make the rub twice (or double every amount) and split the chicken across two trays.

Disposable foil pans heat more slowly than a metal sheet pan, so allow 25–30 minutes and go by the thermometer. Swap the trays between racks halfway through if both are in at once.

Suggested side: baked mac and cheese

A creamy baked mac is the natural partner for the smoky-sweet crust. This batch fills one large foil pan and serves 10–12.

  • Oven350°F
  • Bake25–30 min
  • Serves10–12
  • 1 lbelbow macaroni
  • ½ cupbutter (1 stick)
  • ½ cupall-purpose flour
  • 4 cupsmilk
  • 6 cupsshredded sharp cheddar (4 cups for the sauce, 2 for the top)
  • 1 tspsalt
  • ½ tspblack pepper
  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Boil the macaroni 2 minutes less than the package says, then drain. It finishes in the oven.
  2. Melt the butter in a large pot, whisk in the flour, and cook 1–2 minutes.
  3. Slowly whisk in the milk and cook, stirring, until it thickens enough to coat a spoon.
  4. Off the heat, stir in 4 cups of the cheddar, the salt and the pepper until smooth.
  5. Fold in the macaroni, spread it in the pan, and top with the remaining 2 cups of cheese.
  6. Bake 25–30 minutes, until bubbling and lightly browned.

Shortcut: store-bought family-size mac and cheese trays from the deli or freezer case work fine. Plan on one family tray for every 5–6 people and heat per the package.
