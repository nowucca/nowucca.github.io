Resolve AI · prep plan for the 2026-11-17 tech interview

Prep plan

Seven steps, about two hours, then a short review the morning of the interview. Each step links to exactly what to read or practise. Tick steps off as you go; the ticks are saved in this browser.

  1. Done · 5 min

    Both libraries are in your Excalidraw Plus library: blue = patterns, orange = Resolve questions, grey = canvas.

    • General library (canvas + 10 patterns)
    • Resolve library (Q1 to Q5)
  2. 10 min

    Read for what they sell, who the founders are and what engineering looks like. Skip funding detail.

    • Snapshot: AI SRE, enterprise customers
    • Mission and values: a value to say back to them
    • History: Splunk Observability, OpenTelemetry
    • Engineering: product surface, Labs, stack hints
    • Signals: what to probe
  3. 15 min

    This sets the vocabulary for everything after it. Say each of the ten terms out loud in a sentence.

    • 1. What they build
    • 2. Which patterns they will probe
    • 3. Ten terms they'll assume you know
    • 4. How to run the 45 minutes
  4. 20 min · about 4 min each

    In each card, read the trade-off titles and the default answers. Only read an example when a term is unclear.

    • Write heavy / event driven: back pressure, partition keys, at-least-once (Q1, Q2)
    • Workflow orchestration: durable state, retries, stuck runs (Q3)
    • Search and filtering: index freshness, shards vs replicas (Q1 logs)
    • Events and notifications: dedup keys, fallback channels (Q2)
    • Read heavy: caching, hot keys (Q4 connectors)
  5. 20 min drawing + 10 min review

    This is the most Resolve-shaped question, so do it first. Set a timer. In Excalidraw, drag out Canvas (grey) and fill the five columns. Draw the design yourself before you look at anything.

    Then compare: open Q3 on the prep page and drag out Q3 Agent (orange) next to yours. Note one thing you missed from each deep dive: context budget, read-only tools, speed vs thoroughness, hallucinated causes, durability.

    If time allows, read Q5 Evals. It is the likely follow-up to Q3.

  6. 20 min drawing + 10 min review

    Pick one. Q1 suits the founders' Splunk background. Q2 is closer to the on-call product.

    • Q1 Telemetry ingestion: template Q1 Ingest. Be ready on noisy neighbours and cardinality.
    • Q2 Alerting and paging: template Q2 Alerts. Be ready on flapping, dedup and escalation.

    Same routine: canvas first, your own design, then compare with the orange template and the deep dives.

  7. Optional extra: 10 min

    If you still have energy, skim Q4 Integration layer. Otherwise stop here. Rest matters more than one more question.

  8. 15 min, before the interview
    • Lines worth having ready: say each one out loud
    • Questions to ask them: pick two
    • How to run the 45 minutes: one last look at the timing
    • Open Excalidraw and check that both libraries are still there.
