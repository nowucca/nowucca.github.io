What this site is

This site is a memory of my working life as a software developer and technologist. It holds professional notes, interview prep, side projects, and the occasional thought from a technology or personal angle.

For recruiters

Thanks for stopping by. My resume is here: StevenAtkinsonResume.pdf. To say hello, message me on LinkedIn.

The role I'm looking for

  • An early to growth-stage company (Series B to E) that needs an experienced engineer with strong, loosely held opinions and a pragmatic streak.
  • Education or healthcare is a plus.
  • Someone who can code, lead and mentor, and who thrives on a broad set of responsibilities.
  • A team that trusts I can pick up its stack. Java, Kotlin, Python, JavaScript and C are in my past, but they don't dictate my future.

Please tell me up front

  • The company's name, funding history and stage. It saves me some Crunchbase time.
  • The founders' backgrounds.
  • Where the team is based, and how it works: remote, hybrid or in an office.
  • Why a technical leader would want to join for this role.
